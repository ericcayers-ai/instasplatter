## 1. Rendering

**What exists.** `src/splat/renderer.ts` (1,127 lines → 1,2xx now) is a WebGL2 EWA splat rasteriser:
splats live in an RGBA32UI texture (2 texels each), a per-instance `uint` index attribute selects
back-to-front order produced by a worker radix sort (`src/splat/worker.ts`, 256² buckets), the vertex
shader reconstructs the 3×3 covariance from six packed half-floats, projects it through the Jacobian,
clamps the axes to 1024 px and applies a 0.3 px low-pass floor. `src/splat/post.ts` adds optional
AgX tonemap, FXAA and glare. A separate line/point program draws frustums, camera path, wireframe
mesh and sparse/dense clouds.

**Measured limits at audit start.**
- Every frame sorted **all** splats and drew **all** splats. Cost followed scene size, not what was on
  screen. Measured: 300,000 splats, camera inside the scene → 2,255 ms per frame on a software
  adapter, with all 512 notional chunks drawn.
- No culling, no LOD, no point budget, no chunking. The only lever was "load a smaller file".

**Changed in this pass.** Chunked spatial index (50 m target extent, ≤16³ chunks), frustum culling
from six clip-space planes (`src/splat/frustum.ts`, unit-tested), visible-only sorting, a point budget,
and per-frame counters. Measured afterwards: 93.7% less sort work and 93.5% less frame time in the
inside-camera case; 8.3 s → 42 ms at 1M splats in the wide case where culling cannot help. Full
numbers and method: `docs/PERF.md`.

**Still missing.**
- Per-pixel or per-tile ordering (StopThePop) — needs compute/atomics absent from WebGL2.
- Screen-space-error LOD *inside* a chunk: a distant chunk still draws all of its splats.
- Streaming: the whole PLY must be decoded and resident. No SOG/3D-Tiles reader.
- Depth is approximate: billboard quads share one depth buffer with terrain/water, so soft splat
  edges can leak through a waterline (pre-existing, unchanged).
- The WGSL in `src/splat/pointSplatting.ts` is a reference implementation only; it is not compiled or
  executed anywhere. Recorded in `docs/POINT-SPLATTING-VERDICT.md`.

## 2. Reconstruction pipeline

**What exists (Rust, `src-tauri/src/pipeline/`).** Frame gate → capture-aware poses (VGGT-1B-Commercial
when the sidecar is installed, COLMAP SfM otherwise) → optional COLMAP bundle adjustment →
densifiers (RoMa v2, Depth-Anything-3, COLMAP patch-match MVS, sparse seed) merged into `init.ply` →
trainer (gsplat on CUDA, else Brush) with progressive resolution, MIP 3D filter, AbsGS-style
opacity/scale weights, MCMC noise → optional Fixer polish → live PLY checkpoint lerp → mesh or SPZ v4
export. A separate `sfm/` module implements incremental SfM natively (`run_incremental`).

**Assessment.** This is the most complete part of the repo and it is genuinely wired: `pipeline/brush.rs`
builds real Brush CLI arguments, `pipeline/gsplat.rs` a real gsplat command, `mesh/{tsdf,raster}.rs`
consume depth rasters. The gaps are in the *honesty* of guidance rather than in code paths: sidecars
whose weights are absent are launched and fail fast (intended), and the MASt3R benchmark remains
"runnable but blocked" per `docs/REEL-ADOPTION-2026-10-27.md`.

**Not verified here.** No capture was reconstructed end-to-end in this session: there is no capture
fixture in the repo and the required weights are user-installed. Every claim above is about code
paths, not about a reconstruction result.

## 4. Geospatial capabilities

**What exists.** AOI (WGS84) → ENU domain; catalog DEM connectors (USGS 3DEP, Copernicus GLO-30,
OpenTopography, user GeoTIFF); CesiumJS globe with local DEM terrain; 2D MapLibre satellite/BW;
ENU 3D workspace; `transforms.rs` WGS84↔ECEF↔ENU; GCP-based `GeoReference` with residuals;
`splat_dem.rs` rasterises registered splats to a GeoTIFF; sun/atmosphere models.

**Gaps found.**
- **Precision was not handled.** All ENU maths ran in float32 with absolute coordinates. New:
  camera-relative rendering in the splat shader plus `src/geospatial/precision.ts` with a measured
  budget — 0.03 mm at 1 km, 0.98 mm at 20 km, 1 mm crossed at 32.8 km (`precision.smoke.ts`).
  Scenes beyond ~33 km still need per-tile ENU frames with double-precision origins; not implemented.
- **Georeferencing depends on GCPs but nothing forces one.** `require_metric_registration` exists and
  is invoked on the splat→DEM path; the splat *viewport* still renders unregistered captures beside
  georeferenced terrain without saying so.
- **Globe and splat are separate worlds.** The Cesium globe renders DEM terrain; the splat set is not
  a tileset, so there is no way to see a capture on the globe at its real location. The correct
  contract (`KHR_gaussian_splatting` in a 3D Tiles tileset) is not implemented.

## 5. Positioning

**At audit start:** `src/geospatial/vps.ts` (852 lines) existed and **nothing imported it** — no UI,
no IPC, no test besides a self-fulfilling smoke (perfect descriptors, projections produced by the same
math the solver inverts).

**Changed in this pass.**
- The invented HDOP/VDOP constants (`geomFactor * 0.45` / `* 0.65`) are replaced by a derived
  first-order bound `σ = (reprojPx/focal) × medianInlierDepth / √n`, with the vertical term divided by
  the inlier height-spread ratio. The doc comment states it is not a calibrated covariance.
- Seeded random 4-point subsets were added to the hypothesis set, so the PnP stage is an actual RANSAC
  rather than 7 fixed geometric guesses.
- `src/geospatial/VpsPanel.tsx` consumes submap/query bundles, runs the four stages, shows pose,
  inliers, residuals and σ, and saves the fix through a new IPC command
  (`save_vps_localization`) into `<workspace>/vps/localizations/`.
- Mounted in the geospatial right dock as a "Localise" section.

**Still missing (stated in the panel and here).** Descriptor extraction is external: the panel
consumes LightGlue/SuperPoint JSON; there is no in-app extractor and no anchor-bundle export action
from a finished reconstruction. Without those, the feature is a localiser looking for input.

## 8. Missing functionality, ranked by consequence

1. **Delivery formats.** No SOG/Streamed SOG reader, no 3D Tiles/`KHR_gaussian_splatting` writer, no
   SPZ round trip. This is what blocks ≥10M-splat scenes and puts a capture on the globe.
2. **Anchor export for VPS.** The localiser has no in-app source of anchors/descriptors.
3. **Tiled frames beyond 33 km.** Precision work stops at "you must tile"; the tiler is not written.
4. **Screen-space LOD within a chunk.** Culling is whole-chunk only.
5. **Flood provenance in the UI.** The solver reports nodata cells, coverage, datum and depression
   filling in its result object; no screen shows them yet.
6. **Authoritative hydro.** ANUGA remains a scaffold on a flat bed; the new in-app solver is validated
   against analytic benchmarks but is first-order and uncalibrated (`docs/FLOOD-SIMULATION.md`).
